# Content calendar

The content calendar offers a high-level overview for your upcoming scheduled campaigns.

You can analyze messaging data and view and manage your scheduled campaigns. To open the calendar, select Content > Content Calendar.

## Messaging analytics

The messaging analytics section contains two reports for your recent and upcoming scheduled campaigns:

- Upcoming Message Types: The number of push, email, and in-app messaging campaigns scheduled within the next 14 days.
 
- Messages Sent: The number of messages sent and opened within the past 14 days.

View messaging analytics for a specific day in a report by placing your cursor over that day.

To download a copy of your data, select Chart context menu, then choose your preferred file format.

## Send calendar

The send calendar displays scheduled campaigns for the current month. Canvases, action-based campaigns, and API-triggered campaigns are not included.

Campaigns are color-coded by type in the Key:

- Blue indicates multichannel campaigns.
 
- Purple indicates multivariate campaigns.
 
- Gray indicates disabled campaigns.

note

In-app messages are always action-based, so they don’t appear in the send calendar.

Change the month by selecting See previous month or See next month. To return to the current month, select Today.

View or modify your campaigns by selecting the campaign in the calendar. For more information, see Managing campaigns.
