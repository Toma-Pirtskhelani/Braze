## Choosing a report

 Report | 
 Best for | 
 Description | 

 Campaign analytics | 
 Per-channel campaign results | 
 View real-time results for each campaign, broken down by messaging channel. | 

 Canvas analytics | 
 Canvas performance | 
 View top-line stats, variant performance, and step-level metrics for a Canvas. | 

 Report Builder | 
 Cross-campaign comparison | 
 Compare results across multiple campaigns or Canvases in a single view, with customizable metrics and drilldowns. | 

 Query Builder | 
 Custom SQL analysis | 
 Write custom SQL queries against your Braze data in Snowflake, or use pre-built templates for common analyses. | 

 Engagement reports | 
 Scheduled email exports | 
 Set up recurring CSV exports of engagement statistics for selected campaigns and Canvases. | 

 Custom events report | 
 Custom event trends | 
 Monitor custom event frequency over time, broken down by segment or normalized by KPI formulas. | 

 Revenue report | 
 Revenue and purchases | 
 Track revenue, purchases, and product breakdowns over time, with optional segment filters. | 

 Funnel reports | 
 Conversion funnel analysis | 
 Analyze the journeys your customers take after receiving a campaign or Canvas, including drop-off points. | 

 Retention reports | 
 Long-term retention impact | 
 Measure how effectively a campaign or Canvas re-engages users over time (up to 30 days). | 

 Configure reporting | 
 Weekly email digest | 
 Subscribe to a weekly analytics email and choose which custom events to include. |
