# Analytics 

Braze provides a comprehensive set of metrics and reporting tools to measure campaign performance, track user engagement, and make data-driven decisions. Use dashboards for at-a-glance monitoring, reports for deeper analysis across campaigns and Canvases, and tracking settings to configure how Braze captures user actions. You can also supplement your analytics with Currents, a data streaming export tool for acting on large amounts of granular customer data in your own BI platforms.

## Frequently asked questions

### What analytics dashboards are available in Braze?

Braze includes prebuilt and customizable dashboards for campaign, Canvas, and channel performance. Refer to Dashboards for available views and setup steps.

### How do I export Braze analytics data?

Use reports in the dashboard, scheduled exports, or Currents to stream event data to your warehouse or BI tools.

### Where can I find definitions for Braze metrics?

Refer to the Metrics glossary for definitions of common campaign and engagement metrics.
