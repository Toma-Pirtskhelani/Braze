# Email templates 

Refine your email outreach by building responsive, on-brand templates in Braze. Create templates in the drag-and-drop editor, upload custom HTML, manage URL link templates, and use link aliasing to track clicks while keeping URLs clean. Reusable templates help your team maintain consistent branding and reduce per-campaign setup time.
