Reviewer: Forrest Burnson — Retention Marketing Manager in Marketing at Qwick
(51-200 employees) — Vetted Review, 7 years of experience — August 12, 2026
Rating: 4/5 (Incentivized)
Title: "The best lifecycle messaging platform for app-based businesses"

Use Cases and Deployment Scope:
We use Braze as our primary cross channel marketing communications platform to send
email & SMS campaigns, in addition to using it for automated journeys triggered by user
actions on our app. We also use Braze for ad hoc communications such as In App and push
messages that are easier to launch within the platform as opposed to developing within
our product.

Pros:
- Complex segmentation and canvas/journey tools; feel like it's powerful enough to do
  whatever we need
- great cross channel support for all types of campaigns
- generally good customer support, knowledge base and resources

Cons:
- Uploading contact lists can be a major pain; needing external ids attached to
  emails/phone numbers requires additional processing workarounds if you're getting
  lists from a siloed company source
- AI features feel half baked; would be great to have an AI assistant in the platform
  that can help with repetitive tasks
- the html and drag and drop editors can be finicky at times

Likelihood to Recommend:
Braze is probably the best marketing automation platform for app-based companies. The
integrations and cross channel messaging support are powerful and robust. It does
require having engineering resources on your team to make the most out of it, but the
platform is very 'hackable' in the sense that you can build a lot of useful things with
it that would otherwise require another 3rd party service. Probably too much/too
expensive for smaller businesses + non-enterprise ecommerce.
